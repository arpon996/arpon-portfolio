I enjoy taking a problem, breaking it down, and turning the solution into something people can actually use.

I am currently pursuing my B.Sc. in Electrical and Electronic Engineering (EEE) at Jamalpur Science and Technology University (JSTU). With a strong academic foundation (GPA 5.00 in secondary and 4.92 in higher secondary levels), I have always been driven by a deep curiosity about how things work and how technology can solve real-world problems.

My core interests sit at the intersection of electrical engineering, embedded systems, renewable energy, and automation. Whether it's designing a circuit, writing code for microcontrollers like Arduino and ESP32, or building a Firebase-powered dashboard, I love bridging the gap between hardware and software.

Beyond academics, I am deeply involved in tech leadership and community building. I currently serve as the Assistant Organizing Secretary of the JSTU Robotics Club and an Executive Member of the Programming Club. Alongside participating in IEEE Student Branch seminars, these leadership roles allow me to drive collaborative problem-solving, organize tech events, and stay connected with the latest engineering innovations.
